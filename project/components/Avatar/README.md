A circular initials disc in `lavanda`, `pendiente`, `descartada` or `naranja` — guests, households and the couple.

## Props
- `initials` (two letters, 13px 700 `marino`), `tone` (`lavanda` default · `pendiente` · `descartada` · `naranja`), `size` (36 in RSVP and invitation cards, 40 in the guest list, 32 in the sidebar), `ring` (2px `blanco` border for stacks), `name` (tooltip).

## Usage
There are no photos of people: always initials. Households in lists use `lavanda`.
