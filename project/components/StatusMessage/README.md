The success banner after an RSVP is sent: `marino`, a `naranja` check disc and a two-line message.

## Props
- `title` (17px bold, e.g. "¡Gracias! Recibimos tu respuesta"), `children` (14px `texto-claro` summary).

## Usage
Top of the RSVP form after submit, with `role="status"`. Summarise what was recorded ("3 de 3 a la fiesta · 2 al brunch. Te avisaremos si algo cambia.").
