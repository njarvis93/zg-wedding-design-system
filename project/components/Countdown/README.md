The save-the-date card: `mayolica` with the ghost tile, the ceramic plate bleeding off the top-right, the date and three countdown tiles.

## Props
- `kicker` (default "Guarda la fecha", in `pendiente`), `date` (Space Grotesk 30px; break lines with `\n` or pass nodes), `items`: `[{value, label}]` — three tiles ("[N] días", "19:00 recepción", "2 eventos").

## Usage
Once, near the top of the guest home on mobile. Keep the date to two lines so it clears the plate.
